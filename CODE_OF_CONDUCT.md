# Code of conduct

We expect readable, respectful collaboration in project discussions, reviews and events. Welcome people with different experience levels. Critique changes and ideas on their merits, listen to feedback and credit contributions.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of another person's private information and repeated disruption are prohibited.

Report incidents privately to a maintainer through the repository host. Maintainers should preserve confidentiality as far as practical, review the context and explain any action to affected participants when appropriate. Responses can include removing content, a warning, temporary restrictions or a ban. A maintainer involved in an incident should recuse themselves.
